How it works

🏁

1. Pick up a racer

Found a TB-RACER in a cache? Log it on Geocaching.com and check which race it belongs to.

🌍

2. Move it along

Take the racer to another cache. Every real kilometre helps the car gain distance.

🏆

3. Follow the race

The leaderboard shows the distance, lap and gap. The cars move around the virtual circuit.

About TB-RACERS

TB-RACERS is a geocaching side project where Travel Bug kilometres become a virtual race. Please do not keep a racer too long: every move keeps the race alive.
